Quotes — their details, prices, and customer — are created in the ERP and staged in WebDB, which
pushes them to the SFTP server as CSV files, on a scheduled job every 24 hours, for Boomi to load into Intershop, so customers can
see and act on them in the storefront. The route below is documented for
**Phenomenex**. SCIEX doesn't create quotes, and Leica Microsystems doesn't send them to
Intershop. Quote *requests* from Danaher Life Sciences shoppers travel the other way, to each OpCo's
CRM: see the [quote requests flow](/flows/quote-requests/).

Quotes — their details, prices, and customer — are created in the ERP and staged in WebDB, which
pushes them to the SFTP server on a scheduled job for Boomi to load into Intershop, so customers can
see and act on them in the storefront. The route below is documented for
**Phenomenex**. SCIEX doesn't create quotes; the route for Leica Microsystems is not yet
documented.

Danaher Life Sciences is a common marketplace for the other OpCos' products, and it doesn't sell
them directly: it has no ERP and takes no orders or payments. Shoppers instead add products to an
**eRFQ quote cart** in Intershop and submit it as a quote request — signed in or not, through the
[anonymous quote requests](/custom-features/#anonymous-erfq) customization.

Intershop pushes each request to the central **Salesforce Marketing Cloud** as a **lead**, with
comments that identify the products' OpCos. Marketing Cloud is integrated with each OpCo, and routes
the lead to the OpCo that sells each product, for its own sales team to follow up. A request
covering several OpCos is split, so each OpCo gets its own lead for its own products. How Intershop
sends the lead to Marketing Cloud, and when, is not yet documented.

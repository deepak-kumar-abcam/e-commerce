Products — SKUs, titles, and list prices — are created in each operating company's ERP. inRiver, the
central PIM, enriches them with descriptions, images, and attributes, and publishes the result to
Intershop and to Coveo. AEM pages get product content from **Coveo** only — inRiver doesn't feed
AEM — and transactional data from **Intershop**: list price, unit of measure, minimum order
quantity, and customer-specific price.

The route is broadly the same for every OpCo on the central instance that has an ERP, with some
variations per OpCo that are not yet documented. **Danaher Life Sciences** has no ERP: it is a
common marketplace hosting the other OpCos' products, which inRiver feeds to Intershop and Coveo for
it from the same source. Shoppers there don't buy: they raise
[quote requests](/flows/quote-requests/), which go to each product's OpCo. inRiver also carries some **list prices** today; they are planned to
move out of it.

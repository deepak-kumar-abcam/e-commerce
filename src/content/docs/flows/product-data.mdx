Products — SKUs, titles, and list prices — are created in each operating company's ERP. inRiver, the
central PIM, enriches them with descriptions, images, and attributes, and publishes the result to
Intershop for the storefront and to Coveo for search and recommendations.

The route is broadly the same for every OpCo on the central instance, with some variations per OpCo
that are not yet documented. inRiver also carries some **list prices** today; they are planned to
move out of it.

Customer-specific pricing agreements are created in the ERP and reach Intershop as files on the
SFTP server, which Boomi loads.

- **Phenomenex** stages them in WebDB first, which pushes them to the SFTP server as CSV files, on
  a scheduled job every 24 hours.
- **SCIEX** publishes them straight from Oracle, on a scheduled job every 24 hours that writes XML
  files to the SFTP server over SSH.

Leica Microsystems doesn't send customer-specific prices to Intershop.

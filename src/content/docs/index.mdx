## What this platform does

**Intershop** is the e-commerce platform across Danaher Life Sciences. Our team runs the
**central instance**: rather than standing up their own commerce stacks, four operating companies
launch on it — Danaher Life Sciences, SCIEX, Phenomenex, and Leica Microsystems — each in its own
regions, each with its own branded storefront.

Three more operating companies also sell on Intershop, but on **their own instances**, with their
own code and their own teams: Pall and Beckman Coulter Life Sciences on ICM 14, and Leica
Biosystems on ICM 10. This site lists them so the full picture is visible, but documents only the
central instance in depth.

On the central instance, the platform team runs everything that is shared: the commerce platform
itself, the AEM storefronts, customer identity (Auth0), the inRiver PIM, Coveo search, and the Boomi
integrations that feed them. What differs is everything behind the sale: each operating company processes orders in its own ERP, takes payment through its
own provider, and runs campaigns from its own marketing automation platform. Intershop is the
common commerce layer that talks to all of them.

This site documents both halves — the platform capabilities every central-instance OpCo draws on,
and the integrations that connect each one to its own backend systems.

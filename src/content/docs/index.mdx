## What this platform does

We run **Intershop** as the e-commerce platform for Danaher Life Sciences. Rather than each
operating company standing up its own commerce stack, they all launch on a **single Intershop
instance** that our team manages centrally. Five operating companies trade on it today —
Danaher Life Sciences, SCIEX, Phenomenex, Leica Microsystems, and Pall — each in its own regions,
each with its own branded storefront.

What is shared is the commerce platform itself and customer identity. What differs is everything
behind the sale: each operating company processes orders in its own ERP, takes payment through its
own provider, and runs campaigns from its own marketing automation platform. Intershop is the
common front end that talks to all of them.

This site documents both halves — the platform capabilities every operating company draws on, and
the integrations that connect each one to its own backend systems.

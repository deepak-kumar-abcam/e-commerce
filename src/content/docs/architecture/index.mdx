The central Intershop instance is one part of a wider set of systems, most of them shared by every
operating company on it and run by the platform team. This page maps them; the
[flow diagrams](/flows/) follow each kind of data through them hop by hop.

## How it fits together

- **AEM is the storefront.** Intershop hosts no customer-facing pages. Each OpCo's storefront,
  checkout included, is built in Adobe Experience Manager, whose components call Intershop's REST
  APIs directly. Customers sign in through the central Auth0 tenant.
- **Commerce consumes data it doesn't own.** Products are created in each OpCo's ERP and enriched in
  inRiver, the central PIM, before they reach Intershop and Coveo. Customers, customer-specific
  prices, quotes, and segments come from the ERP and CRM. See the [flow diagrams](/flows/).
- **Inbound data travels as files.** ERPs and, for Phenomenex, WebDB drop files on the Danaher Life
  Sciences SFTP server; Boomi picks them up and delivers them to inRiver and Intershop.
- **Everything behind the sale differs per OpCo.** Orders go to each OpCo's own ERP, payment runs
  through its own provider, and campaigns through its own marketing platform.

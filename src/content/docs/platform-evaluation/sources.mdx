## Could not be confirmed

Open questions from the research. Each needs a vendor answer or the proof of concept:

- Commercial terms for **managed** Commerce MCP servers. The code is MIT-licensed; hosted terms may
  differ.
- Whether commercetools is a partner in **Google's AP2** payment protocol. It is not named in
  Google's launch post.
- Any **Visa or Mastercard** agent-payment integration, for either vendor.
- A general **per-second API rate limit** for commercetools. None is published.
- Whether the **300,000-Store limit** is hard or soft. commercetools' own pages disagree.
- Native **budgets, cost centers, or requisitions** in commercetools. None were found.
- Whether commercetools **Approval Rules apply to quotes**.
- Intershop **order templates, contract pricing, and recurring orders**. The feature lists require
  a login.
- Who maintains the commercetools **Cybersource** connector.
- Marketplace connectors for **D365, Eloqua, Pardot, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud**. None found.
- Any **Intershop → commercetools migration in B2B or life sciences**. None found.

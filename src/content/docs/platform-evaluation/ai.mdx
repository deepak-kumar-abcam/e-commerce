## Agent protocols

Agents reach commerce platforms through a few emerging standards. **MCP** is how an agent calls a
system's tools and data; the others cover product feeds, checkout, and payment by agents.

## What neither platform does yet

Neither vendor documents a complete **B2B buying flow driven by an agent** — one that respects
approval rules, budgets, and punchout. commercetools comes closest: its MCP server can be pinned to
a single buyer and Business Unit. Whether approvals and budgets hold when an agent is buying is
exactly what the [proof of concept](/platform-evaluation/poc/#poc-mcp) should test.

## Our catalog may not belong to either platform

Our product data is mastered upstream: ERPs feed the PIM, which feeds both AEM and commerce. So
making our catalog visible to public AI assistants — the fourth use case — does not have to run
through the commerce platform at all. The PIM or AEM may be the better place to publish feeds from,
whichever platform we choose. That makes use case 4 a weaker reason to switch than it first
appears.

## What every move has in common

Whichever path moves an OpCo to commercetools, two pieces of work sit on its critical path:

- **ERP integrations are rebuilt, not ported.** They are Java cartridges in the shared Intershop
  codebase today, so each ERP (D365, SAP, Oracle) needs a new event-driven integration.
- **AEM changes with every OpCo that moves.** AEM calls Intershop directly, so every AEM component
  that talks to commerce must be rewritten against commercetools for each OpCo.

Product data is the exception: the PIM feed is re-pointed rather than rebuilt.

## What others have done

- **PLUS Supermarkets** (Netherlands, grocery) moved from Intershop to commercetools over three
  years and cut over in a single 24-hour window. It is the only public Intershop → commercetools
  migration we found, and it is B2C, not B2B. <SourceRefs ids={['ctPlus']} />
- **Incremental rollouts** — by domain, as at John Lewis, or by market, as at ISC2 — are what
  commercetools recommends. That recommendation comes from the vendor.
  <SourceRefs ids={['ctMigrationStories', 'ctMigrationGuide']} />
- **Partial adoption** is newly possible: since July 2026 commercetools sells Core Commerce and
  Product Catalog as separate modules. Whether any module could sit alongside Intershop usefully is
  unexplored. <SourceRefs ids={['ctModular']} />

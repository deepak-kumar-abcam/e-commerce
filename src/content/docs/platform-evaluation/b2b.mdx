## Punchout: the likely deciding gap

Punchout lets a customer's buyers shop from inside their own procurement system (for example SAP
Ariba or Coupa) and send the cart back for approval there. Intershop supports OCI and cXML natively. commercetools does not — it relies on a partner
product, Shopspray PunchoutPilot, which adds a vendor, a contract, and an integration to operate.

If any OpCo depends on punchout, testing the partner product is the first item in the
[proof of concept](/platform-evaluation/poc/#poc-punchout).

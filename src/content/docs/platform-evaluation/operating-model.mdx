Today the central team governs one codebase that every OpCo contributes to. On commercetools, the
platform itself needs no upgrades, but our code spreads across several independently deployed
services. Central control stays possible — it just shifts from **governing one codebase** to
**governing many deployables and the API permissions between them**.

## Cost drivers

This page deliberately carries **no figures**. These are the drivers any business case needs to
price.

**Licensing and platform**

- **Intershop:** platform licence and hosting. Intershop offers its Commerce Platform on Azure; our
  own hosting arrangement is not documented here. Copilot for Merchants comes as an *Agentic
  Commerce Package* with tiered, credit-based monthly pricing.
- **commercetools:** SaaS subscription, now sold as separable modules (for example Core Commerce and
  Product Catalog). AI capabilities such as AI Hub and Agent Gateway are add-ons. Commercial terms
  for managed MCP servers were not confirmed.

**Running costs we would add on commercetools**

- Hosting for our own services: ERP connectors, the Auth0 introspection service, budget checks,
  and marketing sync — either in commercetools Connect or our own cloud.
- Partner products: punchout (Shopspray) and any marketplace payment connectors.

**One-off migration effort**

- Rebuilding three ERP integrations (D365, SAP, Oracle).
- Rewriting AEM's commerce components, for each OpCo that moves.
- Custom builds for B2B features commercetools lacks — scaled by what our OpCos actually use.
- A period of running both platforms side by side, for every path except big bang.

**People**

- Moving from Intershop cartridge expertise towards API and cloud-service development, through
  training, hiring, or an implementation partner.
